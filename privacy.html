Privacy Policy

Wolpy respects your privacy.

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app does not require user accounts and does not use tracking technologies or analytics tools.

All wallpapers displayed in the app are provided through the Pexels API. Wolpy does not claim ownership of any images. Pexels is used solely as a content provider and does not receive or collect personal data from users through this app.
